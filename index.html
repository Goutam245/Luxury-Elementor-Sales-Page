Frequently Asked Questions

What if my partner won't participate?

Many participants start the program alone and still experience significant transformation. The techniques are designed to shift the dynamic of your relationship even if only one person is actively implementing them. Often, the reluctant partner becomes more interested as they notice positive changes.

How much time does the program require?

The core program requires about 2-3 hours per week for the video lessons and exercises. Many couples find they can easily integrate the practices into their daily routines. The program is self-paced, so you can move faster or slower based on your schedule.

Is this program right for us if we're considering divorce?

Yes, many couples who were on the brink of divorce have used the Eternal Union Method to completely transform their relationships. The program addresses the root causes of relationship breakdown and provides practical tools to rebuild connection. Our 30-day guarantee means you have nothing to lose by trying.

What format is the course content delivered in?

The course is delivered through our secure online platform with video lessons, downloadable workbooks, audio exercises, and practical assignments. You'll have lifetime access to all materials, including future updates.

How is this different from couples therapy?

While therapy focuses on processing past issues, the Eternal Union Method is a proactive educational program that teaches specific skills and frameworks for creating the relationship you want. It's complementary to therapy but serves a different purpose - giving you practical tools you can use immediately.

What if we need additional support?

All students have access to our private community where you can ask questions and get support from both peers and our team. The Complete Program includes monthly group coaching calls with Dr. Rodriguez for personalized guidance.
